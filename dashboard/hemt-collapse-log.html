Thirteen working days. The model is now calibrated against a measured HfO₂ device, and the late-onset search with that model has pushed the deep collapse from 0.2 V (Run F) to 3.5 V. Oct 7–8 · Electron energy balance (parked)

Replaced the local-field electron temperature with a solved energy-balance equation (Ian's ElecTemperature), with electron statistics at the electron temperature; it runs without the thermoelectric term but not with it, so it is parked.

field mobilityjobs 45185690 · 45186466 · 45189280 · 45193421 · 45197486 · 45202242 · 45207320 · 45208486 · 45209164 · 45209165 · 45210603 · 45210604

Done

  • Oct 7: exported a GaN starter package (model, device, Id-Vd and Id-Vg drivers, quick test) to the flooxs-pyplot repo; the quick test gives 136.9 mA/mm at Vd 1 V, as calibrated.
  • Energy balance behind an eTemp switch (off by default); with it off the trap-free sweep is byte-identical to the old model up to 4 V.
  • Without the thermoelectric term it converges: electrons reach 1,800–3,900 K by about 2 V. With statistics at the lattice temperature the current matches drift-diffusion exactly; with statistics at the electron temperature it rises by +17 to +48% at 2 V (245 vs 168 mA/mm at τ 1 ps).
  • With τ = 0.1–1 ps the late-collapse trap set starts collapsing at 0.2–0.6 V instead of 2.3 / 3.5 V, but most of those runs stop at the runaway.
  • Added the thermoelectric (Seebeck) current term, then debugged it with capped-iteration field dumps: Newton damping and a density floor were added, but the first update still drives the electron temperature to about 0.3 K below the 2DEG in the access region.

Needed work

  • parkedEvery run with the thermoelectric term fails at the first 0.1 V step. Next to try: make the convective energy flux exactly 1.5·k·Te times the electron current (same mobility and floor), then rerun the debug job. Details in progress.md.
  • notedThe +17–48% current from statistics at the electron temperature is likely an artifact without the thermoelectric term; it would also shift the Vds = 10 V calibration.
  • openTrap runs with the energy balance stop at the runaway: the plain solve at the next Vd already fails, so the Te-ramp rescue never starts. Needs a continuation in τ or in the trap coupling.
  • stoppedThree test sets cancelled once they were clearly failing (45193421, 45209165, 45210604).

Oct 3 · Trap level / concentration maps, curve tracer at Vg = −1 and 0 V

Repeated the position-map surfaces and GIF for five more trap level / concentration sets, and ran the key devices at less negative gate bias.

field mobilityjobs 44573468 · 44573629

Done

  • Position maps for 0.30 eV / 4e18 and 6e18, 0.25 / 6e18, 0.40 / 5e18 and 0.55 / 4e18 (hotTau 1e-14, hotEb 1.0; 325/325 runs clean, 29 rescued by the Te-ramp). Each has a 10-panel surface grid, an onset map and a GIF.
  • Deeper level or more charge collapses earlier and more uniformly: 0.4–0.5 V almost everywhere at 0.55 eV, 0.8–1.2 V at 0.40 eV. 0.30 eV / 4e18 has no deep collapse at all.
  • The onset only spreads outward with trap position near the charge threshold: 0.30 / 5e18 surface traps 1.6 → 3.0 V, 0.25 / 6e18 1.8 → 3.0 V (y 0.285 → 1.0 µm), 0.30 / 6e18 1.2 → 1.9 V.
  • Curve tracer (8 runs, Vd 0–4 V): at Vg = −1 / 0 V the late collapses move from 2.3 / 3.5 V (y 1.0 / 2.4 µm, Vg −2) to 1.5–1.8 V, and go deeper (floor 1e-5 to 1e-4 mA/mm). Run F is unchanged (collapse at 0.2 V).
  • Why: the collapse starts at about the same current (165–205 mA/mm) at every Vg. At −2 V the gate limits the current near that level, so it takes until 2.2–3.4 V; at −1 / 0 V the current passes it by ~1.6 V. The peak field at the gate edge is lower at higher Vg (peak Te 335 vs 703 K at 3 V), but these traps sit in the access region, away from it.

Needed work

  • openField-driven trapping (gate injection into surface states, tunnelling into HfO₂ traps, Poole–Frenkel capture, buffer trapping) would move the onset later with higher Vg. Proposed tests: off-state pre-stress with stressFreeze.tcl (no physics change), then a Poole–Frenkel capture option. Awaiting Ian.
  • openMeasure the collapse onset vs Vg (−2, −1, 0 V) on the curve tracer: earlier with higher Vg means hot-electron trapping, later means field-driven.
  • open0.25 eV / 6e18: surface traps beyond y = 1.0 µm reach the 3 V end of the sweep, so they may collapse past 3 V; a 0–4 V rerun would show it.
  • noted3 runs still gave up (2 in 0.30 / 6e18, 1 in 0.55 / 4e18). The local electron-heating estimate for a uniform access region is <1 K, so the runaway must come from local depletion under the trap patch; not yet checked with a field/Te dump.

Oct 2 · Late onset with field mobility, position surfaces, stall fix

Oct 1 · Insulators, deep traps, the dielectric, and calibration

The busiest day: four model questions answered with static mobility, then the deck calibrated against measured data, the field mobility adopted, and the static work archived.

static, then field mobilityjobs 44261474 · 44274780 · 44286932 · 44294165 · 44303974 · 44342967 · 44359813

Done

  • Full x–y trap map including charge in the passivation (339 devices). Filled nitride traps switch near-surface blobs off at rest; positive charge cancels the collapse; neutral traps restore it.
  • Deep traps: thin blobs 25 nm or more below the 2DEG do nothing. The earlier "deep" collapse came from the wide blob's tail reaching the channel.
  • HighK vs SiN passivation: identical collapse at Vd ≤ 3 V. HighK does flatten the field-plate field, but only above ~5 V.
  • Calibration against the measured HfO₂ transfer curve (Vds = 10 V, 200 µm device): the existing field-mobility model fits within ±2.4% from Vg −2.6 to +1 V with no parameter changes.
  • Run F map redone with field mobility: collapse earlier (0.2 V almost everywhere) and about 1 decade deeper.
  • Archived all static-mobility work (folder plus branch static-mobility); field mobility made the default; CLAUDE.md cut from 651 to 276 lines.

Needed work

  • openFraction of passivation traps that are filled. It decides between collapse, off-at-rest and no collapse for near-surface damage.
  • openForward-bias gm too low (88 vs 160 mS/mm at +1 V). Interface charge, contact resistance and surface charge all fixed it only by worsening the −2 V region.
  • openLow-Vd linear region not checked against data; a measured Id–Vd curve would cover it.
  • notedOld fieldpeak.tcl set the HighK permittivity before loading the model file, so its "SiN" run was really HighK.
